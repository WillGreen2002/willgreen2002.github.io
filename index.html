Will Green

PhD Researcher · Engineering Biology

I'm a PhD researcher at the University of Edinburgh, working within the Preventing Plastic Pollution with Engineering Biology Mission Hub in the Sadler Lab. I engineer microbial cell factories to transform waste streams into higher-value chemicals and materials, with a current focus on a next-generation plastic monomer.

My work spans synthetic biology, precision fermentation, metabolic engineering and analytical chemistry. Before the PhD, I completed an MRes with Distinction in Systems and Synthetic Biology at Imperial's Jiménez Lab and a First-Class Biotechnology degree in Edinburgh's Wallace Lab.

Selected work

  • 2025–PhD · Sadler Lab / P3EB
    Engineering microbial cell factories for circular biomanufacturing, from strain design through fermentation, analysis and scale-up.
  • 2024–25MRes · Jiménez Lab, Imperial
    Expanded ferrioxamine chemical diversity through combinatorial biosynthesis across E. coli, H. elongata and P. putida.
  • 2023–24Honours · Wallace Lab
    Developed a single-pot microbial route to dihydroferulic acid using precision fermentation and biocompatible catalysis.
